About ORBC

More Than a Gym.
A Community.

Ongata Rongai Boxing Club was founded with one mission: use boxing as a tool for personal development, youth empowerment, and community. We are not just producing fighters — we are producing disciplined, confident, resilient young people.

Under the guidance of Head Coach Michael Ragot, we have trained young people from Ongata Rongai and the surrounding areas since 2014. Our fighters compete at county, regional, and national levels — and they win.
