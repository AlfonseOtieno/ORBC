What We Measure

More Than Silverware

At ORBC, we do not measure success purely by what happens inside the ring. We measure it by the young man who showed up undisciplined and left with structure. By the teenager who found confidence he never had. By the fighter who competed on a national stage for the first time and discovered he belonged there.

Every fighter we have sent to a competition — regardless of the result — represents months of early mornings, hard training sessions, and personal sacrifice. That is the real achievement.

Where We Compete

The Stages We Have Stood On

National Novices

The entry point for competitive amateur boxing in Kenya. ORBC fighters have participated at this level, putting Ongata Rongai on the national map.

Intermediate League

Season-long competition across the country. Our fighters have competed consistently at this level, gaining experience that cannot be taught in a gym.

Kenya Open

One of the most prestigious amateur boxing events in the country. ORBC has had fighters compete at this level — the highest stage of amateur boxing in Kenya.

County & Regional Opens

Kajiado County, Nairobi Region, and surrounding area competitions. These events have been the foundation of our competitive programme since we began.
